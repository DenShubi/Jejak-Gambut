    About the method

    How it works

    We compare every NISAR pass of the season. The HV signal comes back from leaves and branches, so it drops when peat forest burns. For each spot we keep the biggest drop, look only inside mapped peat, and group neighbouring pixels into patches. The cut-off between burned and not burned comes from each scene's own numbers, not a value we picked.

    Confirmed
    VIIRS saw a fire within 375 m.
    Hidden
    No fire on the scar itself, but a fire within 3 km or heavy smoke, and the scar looks like fire damage.
    Verify
    Forest loss with no fire nearby, or a box shape. Often harvest or land clearing.

    Arrows point from where the first fires were seen to the middle of the scar. Hidden scars get a dashed line along their length instead.

    Limits

    We map peat only, so these totals won't match national burned-area figures. Hidden scars are radar evidence, not confirmed fires. The smoke data (MERRA-2) is coarse, about 50 km, and the NISAR data is still a provisional release.
