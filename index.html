    About the method

    How it works

    NISAR GCOV HV and HH backscatter from every pass are speckle-filtered in linear power and converted to dB. Inside mapped peat units only, the change is segmented into SLIC superpixels, and a Multi-Otsu threshold set by each scene separates canopy loss from stable forest.

    Confirmed
    A nominal or high-confidence VIIRS fire within 375 m.
    Hidden
    No fire on the scar, HV fell while HH held, organic shape, and fire evidence nearby: a VIIRS fire within 3 km or dense smoke (AOD 1 or more).
    Verify
    Everything else: box-shaped clearing, unchanged HV/HH ratio (for example flooding), or canopy loss with no fire nearby (likely plantation harvest).

    Spread direction runs from the centre of the earliest fires to the scar centre. For smoke-hidden scars it is the scar's long axis, so it has no head.

    Limits

    Peat only, so totals are not comparable with national burned-area figures. Hidden scars are radar evidence, not proven fires. AOD is MERRA-2 reanalysis at about 50 km. No deep learning: training on VIIRS labels would make the validation circular.
